# Open Merchant V0 Demo Script (55 seconds)

**0–05s — Launch and open**

Launch Open Merchant and open the bundled “Mechanical Keyboards India” sample project.

**05–12s — Objective and evidence**

Show the research objective, then open Evidence. Point out that every source has a URL, notes, observations, and an observed timestamp.

**12–20s — Competitor comparison**

Open Competitors. Show the five sample listings and call out the deterministic minimum, maximum, average, and median price statistics.

**20–30s — Economics**

Open Economics. Show the five explicit cost inputs and low/base/high selling-price scenarios. Change the base price if desired, save, and calculate the updated margins.

**30–42s — Report**

Open Report. Show the saved decision summary, market observations, risks, and opportunities. Generate the Markdown opportunity report and point out the evidence index.

**42–52s — Artifacts and provenance**

Open Artifacts. Inspect `competitors.csv`, `scenarios.csv`, and `opportunity-report.md`. Switch to History and show the report-generation run and its output files.

**52–55s — Persistence**

Close the project, reopen it from Recents, and show that the workspace remains intact.
