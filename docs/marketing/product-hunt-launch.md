## Pinned first comment (post immediately after launching)

Why we built this —

Every product decision we made started the same way: twelve browser tabs, a
spreadsheet we'd lose, and a hunch. The research wasn't the problem — it was
that nothing held together, and the numbers came from whoever argued loudest
(usually a chatbot).

Open Merchant is our answer: one project folder per decision. Evidence with
sources, competitor listings, unit economics computed on arbitrary-precision
decimals in a deterministic core — no LLM ever touches a calculation; the
outputs are pinned by golden fixtures — and a report you can actually inspect.

Six AI assistants draft plans, evidence entries, competitor rows, and report
sections. Every draft is marked, nothing saves until you accept it, and every
acceptance journals its provenance: provider, model, prompt hash.

Ground rules we refused to break: no accounts, no cloud sync, no telemetry,
no payments, no autonomous browsing. Your assistants only see what you paste
in. Your files stay on your machine. BYO API key — or run keyless local AI
through Ollama or LM Studio.

Free, AGPL open source, Windows/macOS/Linux. We'd love your honest take —
especially on where it breaks your workflow.
